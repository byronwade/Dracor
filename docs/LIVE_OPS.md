# Live Operations

## Weekly Rotating Contracts

New contracts appear on the board each week. Old ones cycle out. This keeps the contract board fresh without requiring players to log in daily.

### How It Works

- Every Monday, the contract board refreshes with a new set
- Some contracts are repeatable within their rotation week (grind-friendly for those who want it)
- Some are one-time community goals (first player or group to complete removes it from the board)
- Completed community contracts are recorded on the deed board permanently
- Previous weeks' contracts may return in future rotations — nothing is permanently lost

### Design Intent

Players who check in once a week always have something new. Players who play daily have enough variety. Nobody is punished for their schedule.

## Public Events

Periodic server-wide events that all connected players can participate in. These are cooperative, not competitive.

### Structure

- Events are announced in-game (town crier NPC, visual effects on the world)
- All players in the relevant zone can participate
- Rewards based on participation, not ranking (damage dealt, healing done, time spent, objectives completed)
- Events have lore significance — they advance the world's story
- Duration: 15-45 minutes per event occurrence

### Example: "Defend Ironvale's East Gate"

- Bandit waves assault the eastern gate
- Players fight together to repel them
- Rewards scale with participation (everyone who contributes gets something)
- Lore outcome: Ironvale's defenses are proven. Town reputation increases

### Design Intent

Events create shared memories. "Remember when we all defended the east gate?" Social bonding through cooperative challenge, not through excluding or outperforming each other.

## Seasonal Events

Quarterly themed events with unique content and cosmetic rewards.

### Structure

- Each season lasts approximately 3 months
- Unique seasonal contracts with story content
- Cosmetic rewards themed to the season (gear appearances, housing items, emotes)
- Seasonal story advances the world narrative

### No FOMO

- Seasonal cosmetic rewards return in future seasons (possibly in a "legacy" shop)
- Or they become permanently purchasable after the season ends
- Players who miss a season are never permanently locked out of content
- The goal is "this is exciting now" not "buy now or lose forever"

## Reactivation Weekends

Special weekends designed to welcome back players who haven't logged in recently.

### What They Include

- Unique community contracts (cooperative, one-time goals the whole server works toward)
- Bonus social features (special emotes, gathering events in town, NPC dialogue acknowledging absence)
- "Welcome back" content: catch-up story summaries, what changed while you were gone
- Town celebrations when community goals are met

### What They Do NOT Include

- XP boosts (no power advantage for returning)
- Catch-up gear (no skipping progression)
- Guilt mechanics ("you missed so much!")

### Design Intent

Returning should feel welcoming, not punishing. The game says "glad you're back" not "look what you missed."

## No Chore-Based Dailies

This is a hard rule. The game will never implement:

- "Log in every day or fall behind"
- Daily quest chains that gate weekly rewards
- Streak bonuses that reset if you miss a day
- Any system where not playing is punished

### Why

Daily chore systems convert play into obligation. They make players resent the game for demanding attention. They burn out the most engaged players first. They are incompatible with a game designed for 15-30 minute sessions that respect the player's time.

### Instead

- Play when you want
- Progress when you play
- Never punished for taking a break
- Content is always there when you return

## Player Feedback Loops

The game communicates collective progress through visible, social systems — not leaderboards or rankings.

### Deed Board

- Located in Ironvale town center
- Shows recent player accomplishments ("Cleared the Old Road — Ashara")
- Rotates to show different deeds over time
- Creates social recognition without competition

### Contract Completion Visibility

- When a community contract is completed, all players in town see a notification
- The completing player(s) are named
- This creates moments of celebration without ranking

### Town Improvements

- Collective player effort reflected in the world
- Example: enough "Ironbark for the Forge" completions = the forge gets a visual upgrade
- Players see their contributions matter to the shared world

### Design Intent

Social recognition without toxicity. Players feel seen and appreciated for their contributions. No one is ranked. No one is shamed for being lower. The town improves because everyone helped.
