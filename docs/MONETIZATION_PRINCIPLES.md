# Monetization Principles

## Core Rule

No pay-to-win. Ever. Period.

A player who spends $0 has the same combat power, progression speed, and gameplay access as a player who spends $100. Money buys appearance, not advantage.

## Forbidden (Never Allowed)

| Category | Why It's Banned |
|----------|----------------|
| XP boosts | Creates two classes of players. Paying players outlevel free players |
| Gear power sales | Directly selling combat advantage |
| Stat advantages | Any numerical edge from spending money |
| Loot boxes with power items | Gambling + pay-to-win combined |
| Energy systems | Punishes free players with artificial gating |
| Pay-to-skip-content | Devalues the game's core experience |

If a proposed monetization feature gives a paying player any gameplay advantage over a non-paying player, it is rejected. No exceptions. No "small" advantages. No "convenience" framing that masks power.

## Allowed (Cosmetic Only)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Cosmetic weapon skins | A blade that glows blue instead of default |
| Cosmetic armor appearances | Ornate shoulder plates, different cloak styles |
| Cosmetic effects | Auras, movement trails, ability color variations |
| Housing decorations | Unique furniture, banners, lighting |
| Character appearance | Hairstyles, eye colors, scale patterns, horn styles |
| Mounts | Same movement speed, different visual appearance |
| Emotes and social expressions | Dances, waves, sitting animations |

The test: "Does this make the player stronger or faster?" If yes, it cannot be sold. If no, it can be considered.

## Future Revenue Streams

### Founder Packs

- Cosmetic bundles available during early access
- Includes exclusive title ("Founder of Ironvale"), cosmetic armor skin, housing banner
- No gameplay advantage. A founder and a day-one free player have identical combat capability

### Patron Membership

- Optional monthly subscription
- Cosmetic perks: exclusive emotes, seasonal cosmetic items, housing themes
- No power: no XP boosts, no stat bonuses, no priority access to content
- Social recognition: subtle patron indicator (cosmetic frame or badge)

### Seasonal Cosmetic Pass

- Earn cosmetics through play during a season
- Paying for the pass unlocks additional cosmetic tiers (convenience, not exclusivity)
- Free players earn the same core rewards — paid tier adds more visual variety
- No expiration punishment. Unclaimed rewards remain accessible

### Direct Purchase Cosmetics

- Individual cosmetic items available for direct purchase
- Priced in real currency. No premium currency. No gems. No coins. No crystals
- "This cloak costs $5" not "This cloak costs 500 Dragoncoins (buy 450 for $4.99 or 1000 for $9.99)"

## Pricing Philosophy

- **Direct pricing in real currency.** Players see the real cost immediately
- **No premium currency obfuscation.** No intermediate currency that obscures value
- **No "just 50 more gems" tricks.** No currency bundles designed to always leave you short
- **No artificial scarcity pressure.** Limited-time items return in future rotations or become permanently purchasable
- **No dark patterns.** No countdown timers on purchases. No "only 3 left!" false urgency

## Player Trust

Monetization exists to sustain the game, not to exploit the player. Every monetization decision is evaluated against one question:

**"Would a free player feel disadvantaged in gameplay because of this?"**

If the answer is yes — even slightly — it does not ship.

The long-term health of the game depends on players trusting that their time and skill are what matter, not their wallet. That trust, once broken, cannot be rebuilt.
