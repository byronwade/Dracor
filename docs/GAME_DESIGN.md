# Game Design

## Core Loop

Log in > Select character > Enter world > Take contract > Complete objectives > Earn rewards > Social interaction > Log off with progress saved.

Every step in this loop should feel meaningful. There is no idle time where the game demands attention without giving anything back.

## Session Design

Sessions of 15-30 minutes are meaningful and complete. A player can log in, take a contract, finish it, return to town, and log off with tangible progress.

There are no daily quests that punish missing days. No login streaks. No "you missed yesterday so you're behind." Play when you want. Progress when you play. Never punished for having a life.

## Contract Board

The Contract Board is located in Ironvale's town hall. NPCs post contracts that players can accept and complete.

### Categories

| Category | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Hunt | Track and defeat dangerous creatures | Kill wolves threatening the Old Road |
| Gather | Collect resources from the wilds | Harvest ironbark from Wolfpine Woods |
| Recover | Retrieve lost items or rescue people | Find the lost surveyor's tools in Ashroot Mine |

### Rotation

- Contracts rotate weekly
- Some contracts are repeatable within a rotation
- Some are one-time community goals (first player/group to complete removes it)
- Difficulty tiers scale with player level: Novice, Proven, Veteran

## Weapon + Memory Progression

### Three Weapon Paths

| Weapon | Playstyle | Fantasy |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Blade | Melee, aggressive, combo-driven | The close-range warrior who controls space |
| Bow | Ranged, positional, precision | The patient hunter who picks targets apart |
| Staff | Hybrid, channeled, area control | The conduit who shapes the battlefield |

Each weapon path will have skill trees in the future. For MVP, weapon choice affects basic attack animations and damage style.

### Three Dragon Memories

| Memory | Element | Personality |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| Ember | Fire, passion, destruction | Aggressive abilities, burst damage, ignite effects |
| Stone | Earth, endurance, protection | Defensive abilities, shields, crowd control |
| Storm | Lightning, speed, chaos | Mobility abilities, chain damage, evasion |

### Combo System

Weapon + Memory creates unique playstyles:

- Blade + Ember = Burning strikes, flame dash, ignite-on-hit
- Blade + Stone = Shield bash, ground slam, armor reinforcement
- Blade + Storm = Lightning step, chain slash, dodge-counter
- Bow + Ember = Fire arrows, explosive volley, heat-seeking shots
- Bow + Stone = Piercing shots, root arrows, fortress stance
- Bow + Storm = Chain lightning arrows, thunder rain, blink shot
- Staff + Ember = Fireball, flame wave, combustion field
- Staff + Stone = Rock barrier, earthquake, petrify beam
- Staff + Storm = Lightning bolt, tempest, static field

## Starter Region: Ironvale Reach

### Four Zones

| Zone | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Ironvale | Town / Social Hub | Contracts, NPCs, shopping, socializing, housing |
| The Old Road | Adventure / Trade Route | First combat encounters, travel between points of interest |
| Wolfpine Woods | Gathering / Exploration | Resource nodes, ambient creatures, hidden paths |
| Ashroot Mine | Dungeon / First Challenge | Multi-room instance, tougher enemies, boss encounter |

### Ironvale Town

The social heart of the game. Players gather here between contracts. The town has:
- Town Hall (contract board, Mara Vale)
- Forge (gear vendor, Orin Ash)
- Healer's Hut (healing services, Sela Fen)
- The Wayfinder's Rest (tavern, Renn, information)
- Deed Board (public achievements)
- Player housing district (future)

## Starter Contracts

### 1. "Clear the Old Road" (Hunt)

- **Giver:** Mara Vale
- **Objective:** Defeat 5 wolves harassing travelers on the Old Road
- **Reward:** Coin, XP, unlocks Wolfpine Woods contracts
- **Why it's fun:** Immediate action, teaches combat, visible impact (fewer wolves on the road)

### 2. "Ironbark for the Forge" (Gather)

- **Giver:** Orin Ash (via board)
- **Objective:** Collect 8 ironbark logs from Wolfpine Woods
- **Reward:** Coin, XP, discount at the Forge
- **Why it's fun:** Explore a new zone, learn gathering mechanics, resource feels tangible

### 3. "The Surveyor's Tools" (Recover)

- **Giver:** Renn (via board)
- **Objective:** Enter Ashroot Mine, find the lost tool chest in the second chamber
- **Reward:** Coin, XP, unlocks deeper mine contracts
- **Why it's fun:** First dungeon experience, environmental storytelling, sense of danger

## What Makes It Fun

- **Visible progress in short sessions:** Every 20-minute play session ends with something gained — a completed contract, new gear, coin, reputation.
- **Social presence in town:** See other players in Ironvale. Wave. Chat. Recognize regulars. The town feels alive.
- **Public deeds visible to all:** The Deed Board shows who did what. "Cleared the Old Road (by Ashara)." Social recognition without toxicity.
- **No FOMO mechanics:** Nothing expires in a way that punishes you. Missed a week? The world is still here. New contracts are waiting.
- **Sense of being a pioneer:** The roads are reopening. You are helping rebuild civilization. Your actions matter to the world's story.
